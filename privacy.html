Privacy Policy

This policy explains how sejame studio handles information in our apps, including Planet10, Planetpop, and future Planet10 universe apps.

Information We Collect

Our apps are designed for casual play. We do not require users to create a real-name account. Planet10 and PlanetPop! may create a random, app-scoped player identifier and record install and session timestamps, app and platform versions, language, game mode, gameplay events, scores, round outcomes, card and ticket activity, settings interactions, experiment assignment and consent state, and ad opportunity and lifecycle events. We do not ask the research telemetry system to collect a name, email address, contacts, precise location, free-form text, or a cross-app advertising identifier.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate and secure the game, preserve progress, provide rankings, diagnose failures, measure advertising, and conduct academic research about the timing of non-rewarded interstitial ads and gameplay. Research assignment never unlocks, rewards, or restricts gameplay.

Optional Research Participation

Planet10 asks whether first-party gameplay and advertising-event data may be used for research. Participation is optional. If you decline or later withdraw in Settings, the app deletes study events that are still waiting on the device, stops transmitting future study events, and stops applying the experimental advertising policy. The full game remains available. Withdrawal does not automatically remove records that were already received and aggregated or de-identified; contact us with the app-scoped study identifier if you want us to evaluate a deletion request.

PlanetPop! uses pseudonymous first-party gameplay, experiment-assignment, and advertising-event records to operate rankings, diagnose reliability, and evaluate the disclosed randomized advertising schedule. These records do not include a real name, email address, contacts, precise location, or a cross-app advertising identifier. You may contact us with the app-scoped player identifier to request deletion of identifiable records.

Gameplay, Rankings, and Local Data

The apps may store progress, item counts, settings, high scores, consent choices, assignment state, and unsent event records on your device. A user who chooses a public nickname may send that nickname, score, mode, app-scoped ranking identifier, and timestamp to our ranking service. Do not use a real name or other identifying information as a nickname.

Ads, ATT, and Consent Options

Planet10 and PlanetPop! may show a non-rewarded interstitial after a completed round. Apple App Tracking Transparency permission is optional, and denying it does not limit the game. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents advertising-consent choices and makes privacy options available again from Settings. When tracking permission is not granted, the app requests non-personalized ads. Google AdMob and Apple may process device, advertising, approximate-region, and diagnostic data under their own policies.

Service Providers, Retention, and Security

We may use Apple, Google AdMob, hosting, ranking, analytics, and crash diagnostic providers. Data is encrypted in transit. We retain pseudonymous records only as long as reasonably needed to operate the services, protect experiment and ranking integrity, complete disclosed research analyses, and meet legal obligations. We aggregate or de-identify records when practical.

Your Choices

Planet10 Settings provides the research-data choice and Google advertising privacy options when required. PlanetPop! Settings provides a direct Privacy Policy link and Google advertising privacy options when required. Device-level ATT permission can be changed in iOS Settings. You may also contact us to ask about access or deletion. Because no real-name account is required, include the app-scoped identifier shown by the app when available.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and remove it when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact us at yeowoon.park@utdallas.edu.

개인정보 처리 안내

sejame studio의 앱은 실명 계정을 요구하지 않습니다. Planet10과 PlanetPop!은 앱 전용 무작위 식별자, 게임 진행, 점수, 실험 배정 및 광고 이벤트를 서비스 운영과 공개된 광고 정책 평가에 사용할 수 있습니다. Planet10은 이용자가 동의한 경우 앱 전용 무작위 식별자, 세션 및 설치 시각, 게임 진행, 점수, 카드·티켓 활동, 실험 배정 및 광고 이벤트를 연구 목적으로 처리할 수 있습니다. 연구 참여는 선택 사항이며, 거부하거나 설정에서 철회하면 전송 대기 중인 연구 이벤트를 삭제하고 이후 연구 이벤트 전송 및 실험 광고 정책 적용을 중단합니다. 게임 이용에는 제한이 없습니다. 광고 제공에는 Apple의 ATT와 Google AdMob/UMP가 사용될 수 있으며, 관련 선택은 iOS 및 앱 설정에서 변경할 수 있습니다. 개인정보 관련 문의와 삭제 요청은 위 이메일로 보내주세요.
