Privacy Policy

This policy explains how sejame studio handles information in our apps, including Planet10, Planetpop, and future Planet10 universe apps.

Information We Collect

Our apps are designed for casual play. We do not require users to create a real-name account. Some features may ask for a nickname or player ID so that scores can appear on rankings.

Gameplay, Rankings, and Local Data

Apps may store game progress, item counts, settings, high scores, and purchase state on your device. Ranking features may send a player ID, score, mode, and timestamp to our ranking service so leaderboards can be displayed.

Ads and Purchases

Some apps may show ads or offer purchases. Ad delivery, purchase processing, and store receipts may be handled by third-party services such as Google AdMob, Apple App Store, and Google Play according to their own policies.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for app distribution, ads, analytics, crash diagnostics, rankings, and hosting. These services may process technical information such as device type, app version, approximate region, crash logs, ad identifiers, or transaction status when needed to provide their features.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and remove it when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at yeowoon.park@utdallas.edu.
